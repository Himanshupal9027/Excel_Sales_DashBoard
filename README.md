# 📊 Excel Sales Summary & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive **Excel Sales Dashboard** built to transform raw sales data into clear, easy-to-understand business insights.

The dashboard is designed especially for **non-technical users**, allowing them to quickly monitor revenue, profit, sales trends, products, regions, countries, customers, and sales channels without analyzing thousands of rows manually.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Businesses often have a large amount of sales data, but raw data alone does not provide useful insights.

This project converts sales data into an **interactive and visually appealing Excel dashboard** that helps users:

- Monitor key business KPIs
- Understand sales and profit performance
- Identify monthly trends
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze sales channels
- Explore customer types
- Filter data by product, quarter, country, and customer type
- Make faster and more informed business decisions

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Profit & Revenue Analysis
A monthly comparison of:

- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Monthly performance trends

This helps identify strong and weak periods throughout the year.

### 2. Sales Channel Analysis

The dashboard compares different sales channels:

- Online Store
- Corporate
- Retail Partners
- Marketplace

The **Online Store contributes approximately 40%** of sales, making it the largest sales channel in the dashboard.

### 3. Regional Performance

The dashboard compares revenue and gross profit across:

- Central Europe
- Northern Europe
- Southern Europe
- Western Europe

### 4. Country-Level Analysis

Country-level performance can be explored through visualizations and interactive filters.

### 5. Interactive Filters

Users can interact with the dashboard using filters/slicers for:

- Product
- Quarter
- Country
- Customer Type

This allows users to analyze specific segments without manually changing the underlying data.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Some important insights identified from the dashboard include:

- **Online Store** is the largest sales channel, contributing approximately **40%** of total sales.
- **Corporate** sales contribute approximately **28%**.
- The business generated **₹6.45 lakh+ in total revenue**.
- Total gross profit is approximately **₹2.96 lakh**.
- The overall gross profit margin is **45.94%**.
- The business processed **2,707 orders** and sold **6,698 units**.
- **Q2** was the strongest quarter in terms of revenue, generating approximately **₹1.82 lakh**.
- Monthly analysis shows noticeable variations in revenue and gross profit throughout the year.
